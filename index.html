About Kalei Solano

Kalei Solano is a fictional artist, and this page is a shop demo for artist prospects. The biography is invented. The pictures are stock photographs standing in for work that does not exist.

In the demo, Kalei paints in saturated colour: electric blue beside coral, sunflower beside mint. The shop sells the one canvas plus a short list of prints. A real artist using this page would answer on WhatsApp. No studio address is published here.
